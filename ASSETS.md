# Source assets and business content

The primary reference is [KT Photography Packages on Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/moe-dl.edu.my/ktphotographypackages/kt-photography-packages). Its embedded application supplied the tagline, phone/WhatsApp number, five collection names, base prices, coverage hours, printed inclusions, appropriate events, FAQs and rate notes. Local HTML copies used for extraction are in ignored `.local`/temporary working files.

All 37 public repositories listed on the supplied GitHub profile were reviewed for relevant files. Most contain unrelated education, character or audio assets. Relevant assets were downloaded locally, and photography assets were converted to WebP for the site.

No stock photographs, invented customer reviews, fabricated shoot names or unsupported experience statistics were added. The showcase uses repository assets; their presence does not establish client-shoot provenance. Confirm business ownership/permission before publishing any repository-sourced asset, particularly the supplied music.

Typography: locally hosted DM Sans, Manrope and Playfair Display from Google Fonts, distributed under the SIL Open Font License. License files accompany the fonts.

The original KT-Photo image is also optimised as public/assets/images/kt-brand.webp and displayed in the landing-page hero. The live embed asset URLs were verified with Playwright; the provided repositories were checked again for relevant brand and photography files.
